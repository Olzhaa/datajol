3. What we use the data for

  • Signing in to your account and keeping your progress across devices. Legal basis: performance of the Terms of use and your consent given at registration.
  • Leaderboard, certificates, classes and exams. Legal basis: operation of the feature you enabled yourself.
  • Replying to feedback.
  • Improving the site (statistics). Legal basis: your consent only. If you do not give consent, statistics are not sent.
  • Fixing errors and site security (error reports, protection against artificially inflating XP). Legal basis: necessity for the operation of the site.

We do not sell your data, do not pass it on for advertising, and do not use it to build profiles or make automated decisions.

4. Who processes the data

  • Supabase Inc. (USA): database, authentication and sending of confirmation emails.
  • GitHub Inc. (USA): delivery of the site files (GitHub Pages).
  • Google LLC (USA): only if you press the “Sign in with Google” button; sign-in takes place on Google’s side.
  • Class teacher: results of your class’s assignments and actions during exams.

The servers of these services may be located outside Kazakhstan. By creating an account, you consent to the transfer of your data abroad. Fonts and learning libraries (Python, SQL, Java, C++) are loaded from the site’s own server and do not send requests to third-party websites.

5. How long we keep the data

  • Account and progress: until the account is deleted.
  • Site statistics: 180 days. Error reports: 60 days.
  • Feedback: until it has been reviewed, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Class data: until the teacher deletes the class or assignment, or until you delete your account.

When an account is deleted, its progress, class membership and exam answers are deleted with it. The account number is removed from statistics and feedback records, so they are no longer linked to you.

6. Your rights

  • Viewing data and obtaining a copy: Profile → “Download my data” (JSON file).
  • Correction: you can change your name in your profile. To change your email, write to us.
  • Deletion: Profile → “Delete account”. The account and all cloud progress are deleted immediately.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can change your consent to statistics at any time via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the site.
  • Hiding from the leaderboard: Profile → “Hide me from the leaderboard”.

Send other requests to {{EMAIL}}. We reply within the time limits set by law. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you may complain to the authorised body for personal data protection of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

7. Children

You may create a cloud account yourself from the age of 16. If you are under 16, create an account only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. A teacher who invites pupils to a class is responsible for confirming that such permission exists. A parent may ask for a child’s account to be deleted at {{EMAIL}}. In the mode without an account, no data is sent to us.

8. Security

Communication takes place only over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes. In the database, each user can read only their own records (Row Level Security). No system is completely secure: if we detect a data leak, we will notify you in the manner required by law.

9. Changes

If the policy changes, we will update the date on this page. We will announce significant changes on the site in advance.
